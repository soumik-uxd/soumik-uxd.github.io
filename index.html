About Me

Hey there! I'm Soumik — a tech enthusiast, problem-solver, and lifelong learner based in Bavaria, Germany. With nearly two decades in the IT world, I’ve worn many hats: engineer, architect, coach, and consultant. I currently work as a Principal Consultant at Metafinanz, where I help design and build next-gen data and ML solutions for large-scale enterprise systems, mostly in the insurance space.

I’m passionate about using big data, real-time architectures, and machine learning to drive meaningful outcomes — and I love mentoring teams along the way. Whether it's building serverless data meshes, conversational AI tools, or even blockchain-based healthcare platforms, I get excited by challenging, impactful work.

When I’m not neck-deep in code or architectural diagrams, you’ll probably find me geeking out over new tech trends, sharing knowledge with my peers, or brushing up on one of the four languages I speak.

Let’s connect if you're into data, tech, or just want to talk shop!
